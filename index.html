Why Miso

Named after the cat who runs the place.

Miso is our resident cat, and this café runs on his schedule: slow mornings, long naps, no rushing. Come in from the heat or the rain and stay as long as you like.

Coffee, slowly

Espresso drinks pulled with care and pour overs brewed by hand, one cup at a time.

Nyonya bites

Kaya toast, kuih and small plates inspired by the Peranakan kitchens of George Town.

Stay a while

Comfy corners, plug points and a lofi playlist. Work, read, or just tidur jap.
